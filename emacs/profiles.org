* Purpose

This is the source of truth for =.emacs-profiles.el=. Chemacs remains the
small profile router. During the native desktop migration, plain =emacs= on
=unseen@flake= deliberately continues to boot the =default= Doom profile.
=desktop-native= is an explicit test profile until Doom-equivalent behavior and
appearance pass CI plus a physical comparison on the machine. Termux Doom and
the Android/Temple native profiles stay separate.

One time a human wrote this. It survived long enough to become infrastructure.

* Profiles

#+begin_src emacs-lisp :tangle ../.emacs-profiles.el
(("default" . ((user-emacs-directory . "~/.config/emacs")
               (env . (("DOOMDIR" . "~/.doom.d")))
               (server-name . "doom")))
 ("desktop-native" . ((server-name . "star")
                      (user-emacs-directory . "~/.dotfiles/emacs/desktop/")))
 ("hackmode" . ((server-name . "hack")
                (user-emacs-directory . "~/.local/share/hackmacs/")))
 ("doomemacs" . ((server-name . "doom")
                 (user-emacs-directory . "~/.config/emacs")
                 (env . (("DOOMDIR" . "~/.doom.d")))))
 ("termux-doom" . ((server-name . "termux-doom")
                   (user-emacs-directory . "~/.config/emacs")
                   (env . (("DOOMDIR" . "~/.dotfiles/android/doom")))))
 ("android" . ((server-name . "android")
               (user-emacs-directory . "~/.dotfiles/android/stow/android-emacs/.emacs.d")))
 ("org" . ((server-name . "android-org")
           (user-emacs-directory . "~/.dotfiles/android/stow/android-emacs/.emacs.d")))
 ("temple" . ((server-name . "temple")
              (user-emacs-directory . "~/.dotfiles/android/stow/android-emacs/.emacs.d")
              (env . (("TEMPLE_MODE" . "active"))))))
#+end_src

Use =emacs --with-profile desktop-native= for the native desktop under test,
=emacs --with-profile doomemacs= for the explicit legacy Doom profile,
=emacs --with-profile android= (or =org=) for the native Org-first profile, and
=emacs --with-profile temple= for Temple compatibility. The default profile is
not switched to native until the migration gate is complete.
